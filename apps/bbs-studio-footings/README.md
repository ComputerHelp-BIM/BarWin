# BBS Studio, footings

**Version 0.4.0 · Frozen**

The first BBS Studio: pad footings (rectangular, polygonal, circular; flat, sloped or stepped), manual entry or Excel import, IS 2502 / SP 34 and BS 8666:2020, cutting plan with yard offcuts, 3D view, and Excel, CSV, BVBS, HTML, DXF, GLB and PNG downloads.

From 0.5.0 BBS Studio moved to reading the steel quantity workbook (walls and columns) and left the footing tool behind, so this version is kept as its own app. Footings will come back into [BBS Studio](../bbs-studio); until then, this is the footing tool.

`index.html` is the whole app and opens from disk (needs internet the first time for its CDN libraries). Changelog in its About box.
