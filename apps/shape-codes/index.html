BS 8666 shape codes

Every code as a bar of real thickness, in the plane it is bent in and from above. Bars bent in more than one plane are shown in one isometric view. Dimensions run to the outside faces. Brackets mark the run-out dimension.

Closed links are drawn as they sit on site: the lapped legs lie against the sides, so in the side view they show only as a cut line, and in plan as a second bar alongside. 51A is the hooked link used on site and under IS codes; it is not a BS 8666 shape.

Figures are schematic, not to scale: bar thickness and bend radius are fixed so every code reads the same way. Example values are for illustration; seven of them (11, 21, 22, 26, 31, 46, 51) are rows from a CADS RC schedule and give the same length.

r is the minimum bend radius (2d up to 16 mm, 3.5d from 20 mm) and d the bar size. Lengths are rounded up to 25 mm.
