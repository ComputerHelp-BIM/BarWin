BBS Studio

Drop your steel quantity workbook

The app reads the typed inputs from your column and wall workbook, applies your rules, and builds the schedule, drawings, 3D model, BOQ and cost. Nothing is typed twice.

1. DropYour existing .xlsx, as it is today.
2. ReviewTotals are checked against the workbook's own total.
3. DownloadBBS, shop drawings, IFC, BOQ and cost.

BBS Studio reads the typed inputs of your steel quantity workbook and produces the bar bending schedule, shop drawings, an IFC model with concrete, formwork and reinforcement, the BOQ and a costed rate analysis. Dimensions are in millimetres.

In this version. Shear walls, core walls and columns in the layout of the column workbook. Beams and slabs follow.

Check before issue. Bar positions follow the boundary and web zones you set; the workbook gives counts, so check the zones after import. IS 2502 radii and lengths are starting values to confirm against your office practice. The cutting plan is a best-fit heuristic. Sample rates are placeholders until you replace them. The IFC file is checked against the IFC4 schema but has not been opened in every viewer.

Changelog.

0.15.0: BarWin look. A BarWin strip at the top links back to all the tools; Barlow type, the steel and oxide colours, in light and dark. Schedules, drawings and downloads are unchanged.

0.14.2: shape 51A redrawn. Its two 135 degree hooks are now bent to the same standard radius as the other figures, so the hook ends wrap the corner bar as drawn; the lap in its plan view starts at the inner hook's cut end, and the note no longer touches the hooks. Dimension lines and lap strips on all figures meet the bar faces again after the thinner bars of 0.14.1. Drawing only: lengths and weights are unchanged. The same change as in cad2bim.

0.14.1: compact bar shapes. The shape-code figures draw the bar thinner and bend it to a tighter standard radius, so the shapes read more evenly and take less room; radii a shape states for itself (shape 12's radius, the semicircles of 13, 22 and 33) are unchanged. Drawing only: schedules, lengths and weights are the same. The same change as in cad2bim.

0.14.0: beams, step 1. Drop the beam steel-quantity workbook and the app reads every floor sheet, recalculates each one row by row and shows it against the sheet's own total before you load it; you tick which floors belong to the project, and spare copies of a sheet are left unticked. Loaded beams get the schedule in both forms, steel by size, BOQ, cost, cutting plan, Excel and the PDFs, and each beam has a page with its spans, bars and shape figures. The reader also reports slips it finds in the workbook: a length that is not the sum of its legs, a number typed over the formula, and a negative number of bars. They are kept as the workbook has them so totals agree; a rule under Rules corrects them. Not yet for beams: drawings, 3D, IFC and editing.

0.13.0: open-tie hooks are now a rule. An open tie has a 135 degree hook at one end and a 90 degree hook at the other. Until now the two swapped ends at alternate levels, so a view straight down, with all levels on top of each other, read as four hooks. The default is now the same way at every level. Under Rules, "Open ties are hooked", you can choose the same way at every level, swapping ends at alternate levels, or 135 degrees at both ends. Applies to drawings, 3D and IFC. Changed: default tie arrangement; workbook lengths and totals are unchanged.

0.12.0: closed links and vector drawings. Closed links (box and master rings) are now closed with two 135 degree hooks round the corner bar by default, in the drawings, the 3D view and the IFC, whichever standard is chosen; before, a BS project drew them with the 90 degree lapped legs of shape 51. That form is still available under Rules, "Closed links are closed with". New download: the shop drawings as a vector PDF, A3 landscape, one wall type per page at a standard scale with a title strip. The schedule and cost PDFs were already vector. Changed: on a BS project the link figure is now 51A and the centre-line length of a link changes with its ends; workbook and BS 8666 formula lengths and all totals are unchanged.

0.11.1: PDF layout fixes. A long site reference ran out of its header cell and over the next one, on every page of all three documents. Header values now wrap inside their own cell and the box grows to hold them. The same guard is on the other places text could collide: the firm's name drops to its own line if it would meet the title, notes wrap at the margin, a long table title is shortened, and the footer text is shortened so it can never reach the page number. No change to the tables themselves.

0.11.0: PDF downloads. Three A4 documents: the schedule in standard form (portrait, laid out like the CADS RC sheet), the schedule with full data (landscape, all 21 columns plus steel by bar size), and the BOQ with cost summary and rate analysis (portrait). Margins are 15 mm; each table is sized so every column fits between them, header rows repeat on every page, and pages are numbered. Buttons are on the Schedule and BOQ and cost tabs and under Downloads; the zip of everything includes all three. The PDFs use the built-in Helvetica, which has no rupee sign, so amounts are headed "Rs".

0.10.2: presentation view, bars now sit cleanly in the bends. Drawing every bar as 20 mm at its true centre made the dots overlap the links. The member is now laid out again at the presentation sizes for drawing: main bars at 20 mm, links and ties at 10 mm, and every link and tie bend formed to an inside diameter equal to the bar size, so each bar rests exactly in the bend that holds it. Bar centres move by a few millimetres as a result; the sheet note says so. Real view, lengths, weights, 3D and IFC are unchanged.

0.10.1: presentation view corrected. It no longer redraws links as plain rectangles and ties as stick lines. It is now the same drawing as the real view, in whichever of the four presentations you choose, with one change: every link and tie is drawn at 10 mm and every main bar at 20 mm (both editable under Rules). Shapes, laps, hooks, bends and positions are untouched, and colour still tells you the real size. The sheet carries a note when presentation sizes are in use. Lengths, weights, 3D and IFC are unaffected.

0.10.0: two ways to size the 2D views. Real view: bars at their true diameter with true bend radii, in any of the four presentations, and now a colour for each bar size with a key above the drawing and on each sheet. Presentation view: every bar is the same fixed dot, closed links are clean rounded rectangles with no laps or hooks, and cross ties are straight lines with small fixed hooks, the cleaner look of CADS RC sections. It applies to the section, elevation and section B-B, on screen, in the report and in the DXF. DXF: in the real view each bar size goes on its own layer (BAR-08, BAR-10 and so on) with its own colour. Changed: bars in the real view are no longer all red with blue links; colour now tells you the size.

0.9.1: the wall views now follow the same conventions as the shape-code figures. The BS 8666 link in the section was drawn with its lapped legs inside the link; they now lie on the sides they lap, as on site and as on the shape code 51 figure, and are placed beside the bar in 3D and IFC. In the Line presentation a short tick marks each lapped end, as on the figures. Links seen edge-on in the elevation and in section B-B are drawn like a plan view: a strip with round ends, or a line with a dot at each end. In the Line presentation the elevation shows a dot where each vertical bar cranks out of the plane. Changed: the centre-line length shown for a BS link rises by about 16 mm because the legs moved; workbook and BS 8666 lengths and all totals are unchanged.

0.9.0: the approved shape-code figures are now inside the app, one to one. Shape codes tab: all 35 BS 8666 codes and the hooked link 51A as cards, each in side view and plan view (98 and 77 in one pictorial view), with codes used by the project marked. One engine draws them everywhere: the Shape codes tab, the figure in the bar data panel (lettered with that bar's own dimensions), the bar shapes on each shop drawing, and the DXF shape-code sheet. Four presentations, the same everywhere, wall drawings included: Outline (now the default), Solid, Solid + centre-line, and Line with a dot at each bend. The earlier Centre-line choice is now Line. Under IS the wall link shows the 51A figure; the cross tie shows the 99 example.

0.8.0: correct bar shapes. The closed link was wrong: under BS 8666 it is now true shape code 51 (five 90 degree bends, the two end legs lapping along the sides at one corner), and under IS 2502 it has two 135 degree hooks bent round the same corner bar with parallel tails, as on your own detailing sheets. Ties now stand one bend radius off their bar pair so both hooks wrap the bars. Bars are drawn with their real thickness and a centre-line (Solid + centre-line, Solid, or Centre-line), on screen and in the DXF, where centre-lines go on their own BAR CENTRE layer; the 3D view has the same Solid / Centre-lines choice. New Shape codes tab: all 35 BS 8666 codes with figure and length formula, from the reference you pointed to; the formulas reproduce all 38 rows of your CADS RC schedule. New: add any bar by shape code per member (Other bars); its length follows the code's formula. Rules gained the IS hook extension (6 x bar size, minimum 65). The app now notes when a wall has more ties per level than bar pairs to hold them. Changed: BS and IS totals moved again because link and tie shapes changed; the workbook method is unaffected.

0.7.0: matched to your sample sheets. Schedule: a Standard form laid out like your CADS RC bar schedule (member named once per block, run-out dimensions in brackets, dimensions in multiples of 5, conformity and rounding notes, header block), in the app, the Excel file, the DXF schedule sheets and the report; the earlier table stays as Full data. BS 8666: link and tie hook legs now come from Table 2 when lengths are by BS 8666, and the crank offset is scheduled as B / slope; the length formulas were checked against nine rows of your CADS RC schedule and agree exactly. IS 2502: default inside bend radius is now 2 x bar size at every size, as your beam detailing DXF draws it (was 4 and 6). Labels: BS projects read "179 H10 01-250 LINKS"; IS projects read "2*19-T8" and "7-T8@150-04" with the mark in a circle. Drawings: underlined view titles with the scale beneath. DXF: layers named as in your drawing (CONCRETE, LINKS, dim, TEXT, S070T_Text) plus MAIN BARS, BARS BELOW, BAR SHAPES and the schedule layers. Changed: with BS 8666 lengths the total rises, because Table 2 hook legs are longer than the workbook's 75 mm.

0.6.0: one model behind everything. Each bar is now a true centre-line with standard bend radii, real 135 and 90 degree hooks and a real crank, and the drawing, the 3D view and the IFC file are all drawn from that same geometry, so a change to any input moves all of them. Standards: BS 8666:2020 and IS 2502 are switchable for former diameters, labels and cut lengths; the workbook's deduction method stays as a third choice and all three lengths are shown side by side. Bar positions: vertical bars are placed in boundary zones and web (inferred from your tie counts on import, editable per bar group, web spacing can be typed). Drawings gained a cross-section showing crank and lap, bars from the storey below in grey, an enlarged section view, and standard shape figures. Pick a bar in the drawing or the schedule and it lights up everywhere, with its data. IFC ids are now stable between exports. Changed: confining and general hooks are named ties; ring and tie dimensions follow the standard (hook legs as C and D).

0.5.0: rebuilt around automation. Drop the column and wall workbook and the app reads members, bars and rules from it, then proves its total against the workbook's own. New: wall and column engine following the workbook's formulas row for row, per-element rule sets, one drawing sheet per wall type, IFC4 export with concrete, formwork and reinforcement, BOQ, and a full rate analysis with wastage, overheads, profit and GST. The footing tool from 0.4.0 stays at its own link and is not part of this version.
